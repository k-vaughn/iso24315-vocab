# inception time

point in time at which a rule (3.2.1.9) can first become applicable whenever all defined conditions are met

<object type="image/svg+xml" data="../../diagrams/inception time.dot.svg">
    <img alt="inception time Diagram" src="../../diagrams/inception time.dot.png" /> <!-- Fallback for non-SVG browsers -->
</object>

Clause: 3.2.4.5

Note 1 to entry: After the inception time, the rule becomes implemented and may be [active](active rule.md), [inactive](inactive rule.md), or [overridden](overridden rule.md).

Note 2 to entry: Rules can be associated with grace periods that occur after the inception time.
