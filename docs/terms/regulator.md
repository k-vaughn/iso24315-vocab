# regulator

[rule maker](rule maker.md) appointed by a [jurisdictional entity](jurisdictional entity.md) to create and maintain [rules](rule.md) within a defined scope

</object>

Clause: 3.3.1.21

Admitted term: competent authority

EXAMPLE: A private [campus](campus.md) can be granted authority by a local government who has been granted authority by the national government to make such assignments.

Note 1 to entry: The term "competent authority" is used in many European contexts.
