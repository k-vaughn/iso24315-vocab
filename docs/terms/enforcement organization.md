# enforcement organization

organization authorized by a [jurisdictional entity](jurisdictional entity.md) to enforce its [rules](rule.md)

    <img alt="enforcement organization Diagram" src="../../diagrams/enforcement organization.dot.png" /> <!-- Fallback for non-SVG browsers -->
</object>

Clause: 3.3.4.1

EXAMPLE: police department for a city

Note 1 to entry: An enforcement agency can be authorized to enforce rules for multiple jurisdictional entities. For example, the city police can be authorized to enforce national laws.

Note 2 to entry: An enforcement agency can be an independent organization or a sub-organization within a jurisdictional entity. For example, a [campus](campus.md) can contract with a private enforcement agency or have its own enforcement agency.

Note 3 to entry: A jurisdictional entity can authorize multiple enforcement agencies. For example, a national entity can authorize multiple city police agencies to enforce national laws.

Note 4 to entry: An enforcement agency can perform other functions as well. For example, an enforcement agency acts as a [rule maker](rule maker.md) when deciding to post [conventional traffic control devices](conventional traffic control device.md) at a scene of a collision.
