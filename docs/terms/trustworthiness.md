# trustworthiness

ability to meet users' expectations for security, safety, and reliability

Clause: 3.5.8

Note 1 to entry: Trustworthiness is an attribute that can be applied to services, products, technology, data and information as well as, in the context of governance, to organizations.

Note 2 to entry: Figure 1 shows the relationships among the characteristics of trustworthiness that are considered within the ISO 24315 series. Characteristics of security include confidentiality, integrity (including accountability and authenticity), and authority (including access control, data privacy, and legitimacy). Characteristics of reliability include accuracy, availability, completeness, quality, resilience, timeliness, transparency, and usability. Those characteristics that are not defined in this document are as defined in ISO/IEC/IEEE 24765:2017. See attachment: Vocab-107_1_Trustworthiness.png.
