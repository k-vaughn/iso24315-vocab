# receiver

[role](https://isotc204.org/iso14812/terms/role/) that is responsible for retrieving, receiving, processing and maintaining a local store of [METR information](METR information.md)

<object type="image/svg+xml" data="../../diagrams/receiver.dot.svg">
    <img alt="receiver Diagram" src="../../diagrams/receiver.dot.png" /> <!-- Fallback for non-SVG browsers -->
</object>

Clause: 3.3.1.20

Note 1 to entry: The material entity hosting the receiver is typically a vehicle, but can be a personal information device, such as a smartphone.

Note 2 to entry: Within METR enterprise view, the receiver is considered to be a part of the [METR consumer system](METR consumer system.md).
