# discrepancy reporter

[role](https://isotc204.org/iso14812/terms/role/) that is responsible for reporting variances between [rules](rule.md) previously received via METR and information detected in the field

<object type="image/svg+xml" data="../../diagrams/discrepancy reporter.dot.svg">
    <img alt="discrepancy reporter Diagram" src="../../diagrams/discrepancy reporter.dot.png" /> <!-- Fallback for non-SVG browsers -->
</object>

Clause: 3.3.1.9

Note 1 to entry: The information can be detected using vehicle-based sensors or through manual observations.

Note 2 to entry: Within METR enterprise view, the discrepancy reporter is considered to be a part of the [METR consumer system](METR consumer system.md).
