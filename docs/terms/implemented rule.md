# implemented rule

[rule](rule.md) that has an [inception time](inception time.md) in the past and a [termination time](termination time.md) either undefined or in the future

Clause: 3.2.4.3

Note 1 to entry: An implemented rule can be [active](active rule.md), [inactive](inactive rule.md), or [overridden](overridden rule.md).

Note 2 to entry: Responsible legal authorities can define transition periods (e.g., as required for the installation of conventional traffic control devices, 3.4.1) when rules are implemented or [rescinded](rescinded rule.md) and the enforceability of the rules during these transition periods.
