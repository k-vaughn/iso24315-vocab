# enforcer

[role](https://isotc204.org/iso14812/terms/role/) that is authorized to and responsible for compelling compliance with a [rule](rule.md)

<object type="image/svg+xml" data="../../diagrams/enforcer.dot.svg">
    <img alt="enforcer Diagram" src="../../diagrams/enforcer.dot.png" /> <!-- Fallback for non-SVG browsers -->
</object>

Clause: 3.3.1.12

Note 1 to entry: An enforcer can be authorized to enforce rules for multiple jurisdictional entities. For example, a police officer can be authorized to enforce national laws.

Note 2 to entry: An enforcer can be from an independent organization. For example, a [campus](campus.md) can contract with a private enforcement agency or have its own enforcement agency.
